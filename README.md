## About the Project

Juba ServeLink is a digital service connection platform designed
to connect customers with skilled and reliable local service
providers in Juba, South Sudan.

The platform aims to make it easier for residents to find
professionals, request services, compare available providers
and communicate directly with service professionals.

Our goal is to build a convenient, accessible and trusted
digital platform that supports local businesses, skilled workers
and customers.

## Vision

To become a trusted digital platform connecting customers
and skilled professionals across South Sudan.

## Mission

To make local services more accessible by connecting people
who need services with professionals who can provide them.

## Main Service Categories

Juba ServeLink initially focuses on:

- Electrical services
- Plumbing
- Automotive and mechanical services
- Construction and masonry
- Painting and carpentry

- Delivery and moving

## How It Works

### For Customers

1. Visit Juba ServeLink.
2. Select the service required.
3. Enter your neighbourhood.
4. Describe the service you need.
5. Submit a service request.
6. Receive follow-up when provider matching becomes available.

### For Service Providers

1. Visit Juba ServeLink.
2. Open the provider registration page.
3. Create an account.
4. Enter professional and contact information.
5. Select service categories and neighbourhoods.
6. Submit an application for administrator review.
